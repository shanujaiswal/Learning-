Why <section> Is Not a Div

A <section> groups thematically related content and, ideally, starts with its own heading. Unlike a generic <div>, it carries semantic meaning that assistive technology can use to build a document outline.

“Semantics give structure meaning — not just appearance.”

— W3C HTML Living Standard

Articles vs. Sections

Use <article> for content that could stand alone and be syndicated on its own (a blog post, a news story, a comment). Use <section> for thematic grouping inside a page. The distinction matters for screen reader navigation.
